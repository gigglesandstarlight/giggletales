Effective Date: July 30, 2026

GiggleTales Library respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the GiggleTales Library mobile application handles information.

Information We Collect

GiggleTales Library does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Location information
  • Device identifiers
  • Photos or videos
  • Contacts
  • Usage data
  • Analytics data
  • Advertising information
  • Payment information
  • Account information

Children’s Privacy

GiggleTales Library is designed for children and families.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The app does not include user accounts, messaging, advertising, tracking, or user-submitted content.

Internet and Online Services

GiggleTales Library does not require an internet connection for its story content and does not use online services to collect or transmit user information.

Stories, text, images, and audio are included within the app and are played locally on the user’s device.

Advertising and Tracking

GiggleTales Library does not display advertisements.

The app does not use third-party advertising services, tracking technologies, analytics platforms, or behavioral profiling.

Purchases

GiggleTales Library does not currently collect or process payment information within the app.

If purchases are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are introduced.

Data Sharing

Because GiggleTales Library does not collect personal information, it does not sell, rent, disclose, or share personal information with third parties.

Data Security

GiggleTales Library does not maintain a database of personal user information.

Because no personal information is collected or stored by the app, there is no personal user data retained by GiggleTales Library.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
